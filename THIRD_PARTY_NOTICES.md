## 2. Japan Foundation (Irodori & JFT-Basic)
The kanji lists and vocabulary frameworks for JFT A2 follow the published pedagogical standards of **Irodori: Japanese for Life in Japan** and the **JFT-Basic** examination standards developed by The Japan Foundation.

- **Website:** https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/
- **Publisher:** The Japan Foundation

## 3. IM Japan Training Curriculum
Curriculum vocabulary and grammar groupings (Bab 1–25) correspond to the language preparation syllabus established for Indonesian technical intern trainees under the IM Japan program.
